EPQ (A Level) · Grade A Completed 2025

An Investigation into Existing and Potential Economic Impacts of Internet Restrictions

A systematic investigation into the severity, duration, and economic toll of government-sanctioned internet shutdowns across Russia, Sudan, Iran, Bangladesh, India, and China. Evaluates structural unemployment, capital flight, and unexpected supply-chain damage using empirical market data and the NetBlocks / Brookings COST tool.

Empirical Findings: In Bangladesh (2024), the non-digital garment export sector lost $1.2B in 5 days of blackout; Iran's 17-month shutdown cost an estimated $1.6B, and its Instagram ban alone closed 500,000+ small businesses; in India's Kashmir 500-day 4G shutdown, rural NREGA workers could not work or receive wages due to required online geotagging.
Methodology: Case study analysis, trade barrier evaluation, and critical assessment of the Brookings / NetBlocks COST economic model.

HPQ · Grade A · Unity3D & C# Completed 2024

.XIA: To What Extent Can Playing an Educational Game Help People Struggling with Dyslexia?

A software project developed in Unity3D (C#) with Tudor Bunescu and Andrei Ghigea. Adapts Flappy Bird mechanics combined with checkpoint letter-unscrambling challenges from a 496-word dictionary to train spelling and phonological recall in dyslexic children without inducing boredom.

Dyslexia UI & Accessibility: Incorporates ACM SIGACCESS research (Rello & Bigham, 2017) using warm yellow-to-orange backgrounds and yellow icon frames to enhance readability and reduce blue-light circadian disruption. Features Scrabble-style drag-and-drop tiles and calming low-volume instrumental audio.
Technical Stack: Unity3D engine with compiled C# code for smooth performance on lower-spec hardware.

Hackathon · AI & Next.js 2026

FocusAid: In-Browser AI Learning Accessibility & Attention Assistant

An AI learning accessibility platform built during the BAINSA Hackathon 2026. Assists neurodivergent and distracted students through real-time attention tracking via MediaPipe (no video data leaves the browser), time-indexed lecture captions, one-click "Catch Me Up" AI summaries, and client-side on-device LLM inference via WebGPU (@mlc-ai/web-llm).

Edge AI & Privacy: Runs local LLMs directly inside the browser using WebGPU acceleration, combined with in-browser MediaPipe facial landmark tracking for private, non-invasive attention analytics.
Technical Stack: Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, MediaPipe tasks-vision, WebLLM, and Anthropic SDK.
